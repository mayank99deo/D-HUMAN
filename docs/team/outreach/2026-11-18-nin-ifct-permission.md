# IFCT 2017 permission request to ICMR-NIN

Draft by the chief of staff, 2026-11-18. **Not sent.** Mayank sends it from his D-Human mailbox.

**To:** director.nin@icmr.gov.in
**Cc:** nin@ap.nic.in, ifct2017@gmail.com
**Subject:** Request for permission to use IFCT 2017 values in the D-Human nutrition app

---

Dear Dr Kulkarni,

I am Mayank Narayan, co-founder and CTO of D-Human (www.d-human.fit), a preventive-health app for India. People log meals by photo, voice or text in 12 languages, and the app estimates calories, protein and key micronutrients. It also reads blood reports with AI (never a diagnosis) and suggests supplements only where results or food gaps point.

We would like your written permission, as the IFCT 2017 copyright page requires, to use its values in our app.

**What we would use:** iron, calcium, magnesium, zinc, folate and vitamin C for about 150 common raw Indian foods.

**How we would use them:** stored inside the app, used only to work out the nutrients in each person's logged meals. People see their own totals, never the tables. We will not publish, sell or share the tables, and will remove the data if you ask.

**Credit:** "Longvah T, et al. Indian Food Composition Tables 2017. ICMR-National Institute of Nutrition, Hyderabad", shown in the app and on our sources page.

D-Human is a commercial product: free to start, with a small paid pass.

Could you please let us know:

1. whether you can grant permission for this use, and on what terms;
2. any fee or agreement involved;
3. the attribution wording you prefer.

If another office handles this, please forward it. Please reply to me at [Mayank's @dhuman.fit email] or [Mayank's phone].

With thanks and regards,
Mayank Narayan
Co-founder and CTO, D-Human
www.d-human.fit
