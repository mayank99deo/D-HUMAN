# Voice: the Say card and the Ask mic (interaction spec)

2026-11-20. UX and retention designer, for the staff engineer building it in parallel. Spec only, no app code.

**The problem (Mayank):** people tap Say and ask whether they can talk yet. Today the mic starts inside Fuel's form. Its only signs are a 16 px icon with bars and the line "Listening in English… speak naturally", and that line shows as soon as `start()` is called, before the recogniser is actually live.

**The fix in one line:** tapping Say opens a large card with one big control. The control is filled white only while the mic is really live. "Speak now" appears only once the browser says it is listening. A tap on the control always does the next obvious thing, and the line under it says what that is.

Code this builds on: `components/platform/FuelLogger.tsx` (Say, `listen`, `stop`, `quiet`, `drive`), `components/platform/autolog.ts` (pause and countdown), `components/platform/VoiceMeter.tsx` (`VoiceActivity`, real levels on desktop Chromium only), `lib/domain/speech.ts` (`SPOKEN`), `components/platform/AskSheet.tsx` (input row).

---

## 1. Three rules that decide everything else

1. **Filled means live.** The big control is a white square, filled with an ink mic glyph, only between the recogniser's `start`/`audiostart` and its `end`/`error`. At every other time it is an outline. That cue works without motion, without sound and in sunlight, so it is the one we rely on.
2. **The big control moves forward. Going back gets its own labelled button.** A tap on it starts listening, then means "I'm done", then means "log now". Keep talking, Edit and Close are separate labelled buttons. A nervous double tap can never undo the first tap: taps within 600 ms of the last one are ignored.
3. **Words are never lost.** Close, Edit, Type instead, errors and a hidden tab all keep the words heard so far. They land in Fuel's box, in voice mode, with "Log this meal".

## 2. The Say card: where it sits

It is a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`, like Ask, so the page behind goes inert. While it's open, lock `#app-shell` scrolling with the same `lockPage()` pattern Ask uses. Call `recognition.start()` synchronously inside the Say tap handler, as today: iPhone needs it inside the tap.

| Device | Shape |
|---|---|
| **Phone, portrait (under 768 px, the touch frame)** | A sheet from the bottom, full width. Its top edge sits 3.5 rem down, so Fuel's top bar shows under a 60% ink backdrop (`::backdrop` `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)`). The person still knows they're on Fuel. Tapping the backdrop strip is Close. Size and place it from `visualViewport`, as Ask does (`--ask-h`, `--ask-top`), so iOS in-app browsers can't cover its top. Bottom padding is `max(1rem, env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`. The square is 96 px with a 14 px cut corner. The tick ring around it is 176 px across. |
| **Phone, sideways (`short` variant)** | Full screen, two columns. Left (40%): the state line, the square at 72 px with a 132 px ring, and the hint under it. Right (60%): the language line, the transcript (it scrolls) and the buttons. The header row shrinks to 44 px. |
| **Tablet and laptop (from `md`, 768 px)** | A centred card in the window, 28 rem wide (448 px), height fitted to its content up to 80 vh, `border border-line-strong`, `bg-ink`, over the same backdrop. On laptops, Fuel's two columns stay visible and dimmed behind it. The square is 104 px and the ring 192 px. On keyboards the square takes focus on open, so Space or Enter is "done", and the hint adds "or press Space". |
| **Entrance** | It rises 24 px and fades in over 200 ms, reusing Ask's `ask-up` keyframes. With reduced motion it appears with no movement. |

**The "Speaking in" row moves into the card's header.** It shows as one line: "Speaking in **English**", then a "Change" button (`link-quiet`, 44 px tall).
- "Change" opens the existing 12-chip sideways row in place, inside the card. Picking a chip saves it to `dh-spoken`, restarts listening in that language at once (`pickSpoken(next, true)`, keeping the words) and folds the row back to one line.
- **The first time ever** (no `dh-spoken` saved), the row opens already unfolded, because a wrong language is the most likely reason nothing is heard.
- The "Sounds like Hindi? Listen in हिंदी" offer moves into the card, under the transcript. Its behaviour doesn't change.

**What's left in Fuel's own form:** voice mode now shows only the box with the words, "Which meal?", "Log this meal" and Cancel, plus a "Talk again" ghost button that reopens the card and adds to the words. The privacy line ("Your browser turns your voice into words…") moves into the card's footer in `text-xs text-graphite`. Which meal it was is not asked in the card: the meal is guessed from the time, as now, and Edit leads to the picker.

**Browsers without speech recognition:** Say doesn't open the card. It goes straight to the box with today's line, "This browser can't listen. Use the mic on your keyboard in the box below instead."

The card's view comes from three facts: the recogniser (starting, live or off), the autolog state (`autolog.ts`), and whether there are words or an error. The headline is set wide (`semiwide`, about 1.75 rem). The hint sits right under the square and always says what a tap on it does.

**Rules for these states:**
- **The header** always shows the language line and Close at the top right (44 px). Close stops listening, closes the card and leaves any words in Fuel's box.
- **"Type instead" and "Edit" are the same button,** named for whether there are words. It closes the card, opens Fuel's form with the words (Type mode when there are none) and focuses the box (§8).
- **"Listen in English" and "Listen in हिंदी"** save the pick, restart listening at once in the same card (back to Starting), and keep the words. The current language is never offered. When the failing language is Hindi itself, offer English only. They never open the mic without that tap.
- **Any error with words** goes to Ready to log, with the error's line in `error-mark` above the transcript. It never shows an empty error screen while words exist.
- **Not every state is an error.** Mic blocked, dictation off, offline, no microphone and other errors carry the "!" glyph (`error-mark`). Nothing heard and Language not available don't: they're limits, not failures.
- **The transcript** sits between the headline and the square.
  - Final words are white, at 1.25 rem with a 1.45 line height. The interim tail is `text-graphite`.
  - It grows downwards and scrolls inside its own area. It keeps the newest line in view, and stops following if the person scrolls up.
  - Past about 160 characters, the text steps down to 1.125 rem. There is one step only, with no animation.
  - Set `dir="auto"` and set `lang` to the picked language.
  - The cap is `MEAL_WORDS_MAX`, as now. On reaching it, stop listening and go to Ready to log.
- **A listening session lasts at most 90 s.** Then it stops and goes to Ready to log, so a mic left open in a pocket shuts itself.
- **Food reading off (`aiOff`):** the card still listens, but never counts down. A tap on "done" goes to Ready to log.

## 4. Motion: one ring, tied to the voice

**The piece:** 48 short radial ticks in a circle around the square. This is the orbit's language (`Orbit.tsx` ticks) in monochrome: white on ink, 2 px strokes, no colour.

**One ring does two jobs.**

| Moment | What the ring does |
|---|---|
| Starting | Not drawn, or a faint 20% outline circle. It doesn't move. |
| Becoming live (Ready) | The ticks draw in once, from length 0 to their resting length (6 px) over 240 ms, ease-out, together with the haptic. This is the only movement that doesn't come from the voice, and it marks the one moment people asked about: "you can talk now". |
| Live and silent | **Still.** Resting length, 50% opacity. Nothing wobbles. Remove the idle `0.04 * sin(t / 600)` drift in `VoiceActivity.level()`; silence is flat. |
| Hearing | Tick length = rest + (22 − 6) px × level × a smooth shape around the ring. The shape's phase advances only in proportion to the level, so it travels while they speak and freezes when they stop. Opacity is 100% while there's voice. |
| Counting | The ring becomes the clock. Ticks turn from white to `--color-line` clockwise from twelve o'clock over the countdown, while the number in the square steps 3, 2, 1. While counting it shows no voice level: new words cancel the countdown, and that cancel is the feedback. |
| Errors, review | Ticks at 20%, still. |

**Where the level comes from (be honest about phones):** `VoiceMeter.tsx` opens a second mic stream for real levels only on desktop Chromium, because on phones a second reader can disturb recognition. So:
- **Desktop Chromium:** the real loudness, with the RMS from the `AnalyserNode` as today.
- **Android, iPhone and Safari:** the recogniser's events. `speechstart` lifts the ring, each `result` adds a pulse, and `speechend` lets it settle. That's coarser, but it moves only when the browser says there is voice. Reuse `VoiceActivity` and drive both the ring and the small bars from it.

**Reduced motion:**
- **Live cue:** the filled square plus the words "Speak now" or "Listening". The ticks don't draw in; they just appear.
- **The ring:** two still lengths, resting and hearing. It switches between them only on `speechstart` and `speechend`, never per frame, and with no transition.
- **The countdown:** the number changes each second, and the ring empties in three steps, one third a second, with no sweep.
- **Entrance:** none.
- **Read it with** the `useReducedMotion()` hook already in `VoiceMeter.tsx`. It's hydration-safe.

## 5. The countdown: one countdown, two triggers

**Recommendation: one countdown, 3 seconds, the same look and words, whether it was started by a pause or by a tap.** `COUNTDOWN_MS` goes from 2000 to 3000. `PAUSE_MS` stays at 2000.

**Why:**
- **One thing to learn.** People meet the same "Logging this meal 3, 2, 1" whether they tapped or paused. Two durations would teach them the app is unpredictable, which is the exact problem we're fixing.
- **Three numbers can be read.** At 2 s, "2" and "1" each show for one second, and most people only notice the countdown as it ends.
- **The pause path needs more time, not less,** because those people didn't choose to stop. They need a moment to notice and reach Keep talking.
- **The cost:** hands-free logging takes 1 s longer, 5 s from the last word instead of 4. Tapping the square ("Tap to log now") skips the wait, so anyone in a hurry loses nothing.

**The one difference, and why it stays:**
- **After a tap,** call `recognition.stop()`, not `abort()`, so the last words still land. Late results update the words but don't cancel the countdown. The square is an outline because the mic is off. Voices nearby in a canteen or office can't keep cancelling it.
- **After a pause,** the mic stays live, as now. New words cancel the countdown and go back to Hearing: a mid-list pause ("2 roti… dal…") just carries on. The square stays filled, and the extra line says "Say more to add to it."

**Cancelling is always clear and easy:**
- Keep talking and Edit are labelled 44 px buttons right under the square, never below the fold.
- Esc stops the countdown and goes to Ready to log, and the card stays open; a second Esc closes it.
- A hidden tab stops it.
- Typing anywhere stops it, as now.

**Keep talking:**
- After a tap, it restarts the recogniser and adds to the words: Starting, then Ready, then the words carry on.
- After a pause, it just cancels the countdown and keeps listening.

**Changes to `autolog.ts`:**
- A new event, for example `{ type: "finish", at }`, for the tap on "done".
  - From `listening` with any words (interim counts), it goes to `counting` with `by: "tap"`.
  - With no words, the caller aborts and shows Nothing heard.
- `counting` carries `by: "pause" | "tap"`.
  - With `by: "tap"`, `result` updates `words` but stays counting, and `ended` stays counting.
  - With `by: "pause"`, nothing changes from today.
- The "logs exactly once" guarantee and the `allowed` gate stay as they are.

## 6. Haptics and sound

**Haptics: yes.**
- **When:** `navigator.vibrate?.(12)` at the moment the mic becomes live (Ready, not the tap on Say), and again on the tap that starts the countdown. Nothing on countdown ticks, errors or logging.
- **Why:** the buzz confirms the live moment for people looking away from the screen. It's silent, so it's private in public.
- **Where it works:** Android Chrome. iPhone Safari ignores `vibrate`. Don't use the iOS `<input switch>` trick to fake it: it's brittle, and the filled square already carries the meaning.
- **Guard:** wrap it in try/catch, and only call it after the Say tap, which gives Chrome the user activation it needs.

**Start tone: no, not in this release.**
- **The cue is on screen:** the person has just tapped and is looking at the card. A filled square and "Speak now" answer "is it live?" at the moment they ask.
- **It can't be relied on:** iPhone's silent switch mutes Web Audio for many people, so the tone wouldn't be there anyway.
- **It may break recognition (inferred, untested):** playing audio while recognition holds the mic can change the audio routing on iPhone and stop recognition. A tone played after the mic is live can also be picked up as sound.
- **Some Android phones already beep** when the browser starts listening (inferred, depends on the device). A second tone would double it.
- **Privacy:** people log food in canteens, offices and buses. A beep tells everyone nearby that they're logging what they eat. This is health data; we stay quiet by default.
- **Revisit** only if §10's numbers show people still waiting before they speak. It would then be an opt-in setting, off by default.

## 7. The Ask mic

Ask counts questions, so **the mic fills the box and never sends.** It's compact: no card, no countdown.

**Placement:** in the input row, between the box and Ask: `[ box (flex-1) ][ mic 44×44 ][ Ask ]`, with an 8 px gap.
- **Size at 390 px:** the box keeps about 226 px.
- **Style:** a square `button-ghost` with the mic glyph. It has no cut corner, because only Ask is primary.
- **At the limit** (no Ask button): `[ box ][ mic ]`. The mic still helps people find a screen by voice.
- **Hidden when:**
  - the browser has no speech recognition (the keyboard's own mic remains);
  - the consent notice replaces the box;
  - Ask is off or paused.
- **Its language:** the saved `dh-spoken` pick, else the read language (`hi` gives हिंदी, `hinglish` gives Hinglish), else English.

**States.** Status words go in one line just above the box, where `ErrorLine` sits, with `text-sm`. Errors use `error-mark`.

| State | Mic button | Line above the box | Ask button |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idle | Outline glyph. `aria-label` "Speak your question" | (none) | As today |
| Starting | Outline, `aria-busy` | Getting your mic… | Disabled |
| Live | **Filled white, ink glyph** (the same "filled means live" rule) | [the five small bars from `VoiceMeter`] Listening in English. Tap the mic when you're done. · **Change** (opens the chip row above the box; a pick restarts in that language) | Disabled. A half-heard question would use one of today's questions. |
| Words arriving | Filled | Same line | Disabled |
| Stopped with words (a tap on the mic, or 3 s of silence after words) | Outline | Check your question, then tap Ask. (It goes once they type, send or tap the mic.) | Enabled |
| Nothing heard | Outline | ! Didn't hear anything. Try again, or type it. | As today |
| Blocked | Outline | ! Your mic is blocked for this site. Type your question instead. | As today |
| Dictation off | Outline | ! This device couldn't start listening. Try again, or type your question. On iPhone, Dictation must be on. | As today |
| Language | Outline | This browser can't listen in தமிழ். · Listen in English · Listen in हिंदी | As today |
| Offline | Outline | ! You're offline. Type your question, and ask when you're back online. | As today |
| Too long | Stops at `questionMaxChars` | The existing "Keep it under … characters." | As today |
| Other error | Outline | ! Listening stopped. Try again, or type it. | As today |

**Rules for the Ask mic:**
- **Words add to the draft,** after a space, and go through the same `{ type: "type" }` dispatch. The draft is saved to sessionStorage as now.
- **Typing** while listening stops the mic: their typing wins.
- **Auto-stop:** listening stops by itself after 3 s of silence once there are words. That's a little longer than Fuel's 2 s, because people think mid-question. It never sends. Nothing heard follows Fuel's 10 s cap.
- **The mic stops** when the sheet closes, a question is sent, the tab is hidden, or a "Jump to" link is followed.
- **Focus:**
  - While listening, focus stays on the mic, so no keyboard opens over the sheet.
  - When it stops with words, use the existing `focusFor(true)`. On touch, that focuses the Ask button: one more tap sends, and no keyboard opens. On laptops, it focuses the box with the cursor at the end.
- **Haptics:** the same as Fuel (live and stop). No tone.

## 8. Accessibility

- **The Say card:**
  - A `<dialog>` with `aria-labelledby` on a fixed sr-only title, "Say what you ate".
  - Focus goes to the square when it opens. Close is reachable by Tab.
  - Esc: during the countdown it goes to Ready to log; otherwise it closes, keeping the words.
  - When it closes, focus returns to Say, except for Edit and Type instead (below).
- **The square is a `<button>`** with a label for each state:
  - "Getting your mic", while it does nothing;
  - "Done speaking" while live;
  - "Log now" while counting;
  - "Keep talking" in review;
  - "Try again" after Nothing heard.
  - In blocked, offline, dictation-off and language states it's `aria-disabled`, and the buttons below carry the way out.
- **Live region** (`role="status"`, polite, one sr-only line):
  - **What it says:** "Getting your mic." Then "Listening. Speak now." Then, at the countdown, once: "Logging this meal in 3 seconds. Choose Keep talking or Edit to stop it." Then each state's headline.
  - **Never** announce the transcript as it grows, and never announce each second.
  - **Known catch:** a screen reader speaking "Speak now" can be picked up by the mic. Keep that announcement to those words. Ask the engineer to drop one exact leading "speak now" from a transcript, which is cheap and harmless.
  - **The countdown is a time limit (WCAG 2.2.1).** It is announced, any button or Esc stops it, and the tap that starts it is the person's own. Whether to add a "Check before logging" setting that turns auto-log off is in §11.
- **Targets:**
  - The square is 96–104 px (72 px sideways).
  - Every button, the language chips (`choice`), "Change" and Close are at least 44 px tall.
  - The Ask mic is 44 × 44.
  - Keep Keep talking and Edit 12 px apart: they do different things.
- **The iPhone keyboard:**
  - The card has no text field, so no keyboard ever opens over it.
  - Edit and Type instead must close the dialog and call `box.focus()` in the same tap handler, with no `await` or effect in between. iPhone opens the keyboard only for a focus inside the tap.
  - Override the dialog's return-focus-to-opener for those two: close first, then focus the box, then put the cursor at the end.
- **Order with the orbit:** `MealOrbit` is a fixed `z-50` layer, not a top-layer dialog. Close the card before setting `run`, or the card would sit on top of the orbit.
- **Contrast:** the hint and extra lines use `text-graphite` (6.2:1 on ink). The number in the square is ink on white, or white on ink in the outline state.

## 10. How we'll know it worked

The goal is fewer "am I ready?" questions and more meals logged by voice that people keep, not time spent on the card.

- **Count voice meals by how they ended.** Add one field to the `/api/food` form for voice meals, `voice_end`: `pause`, `tap`, `log_now` or `edited`. Put it in the existing server log line. It needs no new table and no personal data.
- **Watch for two weeks:**
  - **"Nothing heard" per card opened.** This is the main signal for "didn't know when to speak". Expect it to fall.
  - **Voice meals per voice card opened.** Expect it to rise.
  - **The share of voice meals deleted within 10 minutes.** A rise would mean the 3-2-1 logs things people didn't mean.
  - **Mayank's own inbox:** do people stop asking "can I speak now?"
- **On a device before calling it done:**
  - 390 px Android Chrome and iPhone Safari, including the Google app's browser.
  - Reduced motion on.
  - First use with the permission prompt; mic denied; Tamil on iPhone; airplane mode.

## 11. For Mayank to decide

1. **One 3-second countdown for both paths** (my recommendation). It makes hands-free logging 1 s slower; tapping skips the wait. The alternative is 3 s after a tap and 2 s after a pause.
2. **No start tone in this release** (my recommendation): haptic on Android and a clear visual live state. The tone could come back later as an opt-in setting.
3. **A "Check before logging" setting** that turns auto-log off, so every voice meal stops at Ready to log. This matters for screen reader users and anyone who finds 3 s short (WCAG 2.2.1). I'd add it to Settings in the next pass, off by default.

## 12. Never

- "Speak now" before `start` or `audiostart`.
- Motion that runs without voice (except the one 240 ms draw-in at the live moment).
- An accent colour, a red "recording" dot or a pulsing halo.
- An auto-send in Ask.
- A tone by default.
- Losing words on close, an error or a hidden tab.
- The word "accurate".
