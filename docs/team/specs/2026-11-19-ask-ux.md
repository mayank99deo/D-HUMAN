## 0. In one paragraph

Ask is a short-answer helper for signed-in people. It covers four things: food and diet, exercise and gym training, general health, and the D-Human app itself. It knows the person's profile, today's meals, their usual day, their stack and their latest report. It answers in about 80 words or fewer, in the language of the question, and can offer up to two buttons into the app. Each person gets 5 questions a day. It never diagnoses, never doses beyond the stack, and leaves flagged markers to a doctor in fixed words that code owns, not the AI. It lives behind a quiet "Ask" control in the top bar and a few "Ask about this" links next to the things people wonder about.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Priya, 40, regular runner, Indore | 9 pm, dinner logged, long run at 6 am | "What should I eat before tomorrow's 18K?" | Two lines: a light carb snack, timing. A "Log a meal" button. |

## 2. Name

Keep **Ask**. It joins the app's other verbs on Fuel (Snap, Say, Type). It is one short word that fits a 320 px top bar, and it reads the same to Hinglish readers. It promises nothing it can't keep. No persona name, no avatar, no "coach" and no "assistant": those suggest a person or a qualification.

## 3. Entry points

### 3.1 The main entry: an "Ask" control in the top bar

The top bar already holds the wordmark (left) and the profile initial (right). Ask sits just left of the initial, as a right-aligned pair.

| Device | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Phone, portrait (< 768 px) | An outline control, 32 px tall inside a 44 px tap area: the word "Ask" (text-sm, semiwide, paper). Hairline `line-strong` border, matching the initial's square. Its top-left corner carries the brand's cut notch as a hairline diagonal; the `graphic-designer` draws it. |
| Phone, sideways (`short:max-lg`) | Same control, from `md` the wider form below. The top bar scrolls away here (`short:max-lg:absolute`), so Ask scrolls away with it. That's fine: typing sideways leaves almost no screen, and the in-page links still work. |
| Tablet (768–1023 px) | A field-shaped button, 36 px tall inside 44, `w-80`, `border-line`, graphite text: "Ask about food, training or the app". It is a button that looks like a field, not a real input. |
| Laptop (≥ 1024 px, top bar starts after the rail) | The same field-shaped button, `w-80` (`xl:w-96`), plus a `/` key hint at its right end. The hint shows only where `(hover: hover)`. |

**Never on it:** a count, a badge, a dot, a pulse, a sparkle icon or any colour.

**Why not a sixth tab.** The tab bar is a grid of five places, and at 320 px each tab is already about 64 px wide. Ask isn't a place; it's a tool you use on top of whichever place you're in. A sixth tab would shrink every label for something used a few times a day.

**Why not a floating bubble.** It's the most templated SaaS pattern there is. It also covers content and the Settings save bar, and it fights the tab bar for the thumb.

**Why the top-right is acceptable for thumbs.** Ask is an occasional, deliberate action (at most 5 a day); the daily action, Log a meal, stays in the tab bar and on Home. Once the sheet opens, the input sits at the bottom, under the thumb. The in-page links (3.2) put Ask right where the question comes up.

**Where it isn't:** sign-in, onboarding, and the full-screen overlays (the report-reading orbit, the meal orbit, the wraps viewer). Those already cover the top bar.

### 3.2 "Ask about this": context links, few and targeted

Each link is a `link-quiet text-sm` row, at least 44 px tall. Tapping one opens the sheet with the box **prefilled, not sent**, and an "About" chip above the box. Opening never costs a question; only sending does.

| Where | Shown on | Link text | Box prefilled with | Chip |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Report page, `MarkerRow` | Markers that are low, high, at the edge, or flagged. Not in-range ones, so the page stays calm. | Ask about this | Not flagged: starter `marker_mean`. Flagged: starter `ask_doctor`. | About: {marker name} |
| Home, "What matters" `MarkerTile` | Same rule | Ask about this | Same | Same |
| Stack, each included item card, after its "why" line | Every item | Ask about this | Starter `why_item` | About: {nutrient} |
| Fuel, the card of a meal just logged | That card only, not every meal row | Ask about this meal | Starter `meal_better` | About: {meal title} |
| Fuel, under "biggest gaps" | When gaps show | Ask how to close these | Starter `gaps` | About: your food gaps |

No context links on Upload, Settings, Wraps, Past meals or Your reports. There, the top-bar control and its starters are enough.

### 3.3 Keyboard

On a laptop, `/` opens Ask, unless focus is in a text field or another dialog (the tour) is open. Esc closes it.

### 3.4 Discovery

- **New users:** one tour stop, after Stack. Mark the control `data-tour="ask"`; the card sits below it. Copy is in section 10.
- **Existing users:** nothing beyond the control and the context links. No "New" tag, no banner, no email.

## 4. The surface

### 4.1 Shape by device

| Device | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| Phone, portrait | A full-screen native `<dialog>` (`showModal`, like the tour), in the same family as the meal orbit and wraps viewer. It slides up 16 px and fades in over 200 ms. Its content column fills the width (16 px gutters). |
| Phone, sideways | Full screen with a compact header (44 px). While the keyboard is open (visible height under 260 px), only the header, the thread and the input row show. The lead text, the first-use line and the disclosure hide until the keyboard closes. |
| Tablet | Full screen, content column `max-w-xl`, centred. |
| Laptop | A right-hand panel, `w-[26rem]` (`xl:w-[28rem]`), full height, `border-l border-line-strong`, `bg-ink`. A scrim `bg-ink/60` covers the rest. It slides in 24 px from the right over 200 ms. It is modal: one focus model on every device, and the "About" chip carries the context, so nobody needs the page behind it. |

**What every size shares:**
- **Close:** Esc and Android's back gesture close it, as `<dialog>` already does for the tour. Focus returns to whatever opened it.
- **Background:** `#app-shell` stops scrolling while it's open. Add `overflow: hidden` while it's open, so iOS can't scroll the page through it.
- **Closing never cancels a question:** an answer that lands while the sheet is shut is there on reopening.
- **Where it mounts:** the app layout, in its own `<Suspense>` with no fallback (like `TourForUser`). Its code loads lazily (section 13).

### 4.2 Anatomy, top to bottom

1. **Header** (56 px; 44 px when sideways), with `padding-top: env(safe-area-inset-top)`:
   - Title "Ask" (`text-lg font-bold semiwide`, `id="ask-title"`).
   - The counter (`text-sm text-graphite`, right side).
   - "Close" as text (44 × 44 target).
2. **Pinned lines,** zero to two, each only when it applies (copy in section 10):
   - **Urgent hold:** a marker on the latest report has an urgent flag. Shown with the "!" glyph (`error-mark`) and a full white border.
   - **Someone else's report:** the latest report is printed for someone else (`printedFor`). A plain line plus "Manage reports".
3. **Thread** (`flex-1 overflow-y-auto overscroll-contain`): today's turns, oldest first. Each turn is an `<li>` with `border-t border-line` and `py-5`:
   - The question: `font-semibold`, paper, marked up as an `<h3>`, `dir="auto"`.
   - The notice block, if any (section 8).
   - The eyebrow "AI answer" (`text-xs text-graphite`).
   - The answer: `text-[0.9375rem] leading-relaxed`, `dir="auto"`, with `lang` from the turn. Plain text only; line breaks are kept and nothing else is rendered (no markdown, no links inside the text).
   - Up to 2 link buttons (`button-ghost h-11`), labels from the fixed table in section 14.
   - **No chat bubbles, no left/right alignment, no avatars, no timestamps.** It reads as a page of notes, like the report's pointers.
4. **After the last turn:** one line, `text-sm text-graphite`: "Questions clear after today." followed by the link "Clear now" (`link-quiet`, min 44 px tall).
5. **Empty state** (no turns today), in place of the thread:
   - The lead line.
   - The "doesn't use" line.
   - A "Try one" label and 3 starters: full-width rows, `border-t border-line`, `min-h-11`, left-aligned text, no chips or pills.
   - The first-use line, until the person's first-ever question.
6. **"About" chip,** when opened from a context link. It sits just above the box: `border border-line px-3 h-9 text-sm`, "About: {name}". For a marker, its status glyph (`glyph-in/edge/out`) comes first; that's the only colour on the surface outside notices. A remove control "×" has a 44 × 44 hit area and `aria-label="Remove {name} from this question"`.
7. **Jump to,** a free row above the box while typing (section 6).
8. **Input row:**
   - **The box:** a textarea in `field` style, 16 px text, 1 row growing to 4, then it scrolls. `enterKeyHint="send"`, `dir="auto"`, `maxLength=300`, placeholder "Ask about food, training or the app".
   - **The Send button:** `button-primary h-11 px-5`, labelled "Ask", with the cut corner.
   - **Under both:** the disclosure line (`text-xs text-graphite`).
   - **Errors:** shown just above the box, `error-mark text-sm`, `role="alert"`.

### 4.4 The main flow, step by step

1. The person taps "Ask", or a context link.
2. The sheet opens. If the thread is already in memory it shows at once; `GET /api/ask?from=<path>` refreshes it.
3. They tap a starter, which fills the box without sending, or they type.
4. They tap "Ask", or press Enter. A turn appears at once with their question and "Working on it…". The box clears and stays usable for typing the next question, but Send is disabled until this answer lands.
5. The answer lands:
   - The thread scrolls so the **question's top** is in view, so they read the answer from its start.
   - The counter updates.
   - A screen reader hears the answer (section 12).
6. A link button closes the sheet and navigates. If they're already on that screen, it just closes, and scrolls to the anchor if there is one.
7. On reopening, the thread is still there.
8. After midnight IST the thread is gone and 5 new questions are ready.

## 5. Starters

**What they are:** fixed strings picked by code, never written by AI, costing nothing until sent. `GET /api/ask` returns them for the screen in the person's read language (`profiles.language`: en, hi, hinglish), so the answer comes back in that language too. Slots are filled by code: marker and nutrient names in English from the catalogue, numbers from today's totals.

**Rules:**
- Show 3: the first two that apply from the screen's list, then the persona starter.
- Starters show only in the empty state, never after an answer.
- Tapping one fills the box, replacing what's there.
  - **Touch:** focus moves to the Ask button, so no keyboard pops over the screen and one more tap sends.
  - **Laptop:** focus moves to the box with the cursor at the end.
- A flagged marker is never offered as a food question; it gets `ask_doctor` instead.

| Screen (path) | Candidates, in order |
| --- | --- |
| Home (`/home`, `/reports`), with a ready report | `ask_doctor` (first flagged marker), else `eat_for` (first priority marker that's low or edge and not flagged); then `matters`; then `retest` |
| Report (`/reports/[id]`) | Same as Home with a report |
| Home, no ready report | `report_shows`, `how_upload` |
| Fuel (`/fuel`, `/fuel/past`) | `protein_left` (only when 10 g or more is left; rounded to the nearest 5), else `how_counted` (no meals ever); then `next_meal` (at least 1 meal today), else `gaps` (gaps exist) |

## 6. Jump to: finding a screen, free

Mayank's brief asks for "finding anything or searching features". Most of that is navigation, and navigation shouldn't cost a question or an AI call.

**How it works:**
- While someone types, code matches the text against a fixed keyword table. The row appears only when the text is 5 words or fewer and a keyword matches a whole word, case-insensitive, with at least 3 letters.
- When it matches, a row appears above the box: "Jump to", then up to 2 `button-ghost h-11` buttons from section 14's labels.
- Tapping a button closes the sheet and navigates. It never counts.
- Enter still sends the question; the jump only happens on tap.
- At the limit, the box stays for jumping only: Enter takes them to the first match.

| Destination | Keywords (en, Hinglish, Hindi) |

## 8. Kinds of reply, how they look, and what counts

**Safety notices are code, not AI.** The server sends a `notice` with fixed words from `lib/domain/safety.ts` (or the fixed lines in section 10). The model never writes them, and the client renders them apart from the AI text, above it.

| Kind | Look | AI text | Counts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `answer` | Plain | Yes, at most about 80 words | Yes |
| `off_topic` | Graphite text | One line, at most 25 words, in the question's language. It never answers the off-topic part. | Recommended yes (open question 1) |
| `doctor` | **Block:** `border border-line-strong p-4`, a faint haem wash (`rgb(227 165 162 / 0.10)`). Label row: the white "!" square from `VerdictMark` with "Doctor first". A named marker carries its status glyph before its name. | Optional. It may say what the marker is and what to ask a doctor. No food or supplement "fix" for a flagged marker, and no dose. | Yes |
| `urgent` | **Block:** `border border-paper p-4`, haem wash at 0.16. Label row: the "!" square with "Get advice today". For a symptom, in India: a "Call 112" `button-ghost h-11` (`tel:112`). | None | No |
| `crisis` | **Block:** `border border-paper p-4`, no wash and no "!". Calm on purpose. Buttons "Call 14416" and "Call 112" (`tel:`), in India only. | None | No |
| `limit` | Section 7 | None | n/a |

**Never counted:** a send that never reached us, timeout without a landed answer, errors, refusals, report deleted mid-answer. A retry with the same `id` never counts twice, so a question whose answer was lost on the way counts once, however often it's sent again (2026-11-22).

**When the server returns `doctor` or `urgent` instead of `answer`:**
- **A flagged marker:** the question or its "About" context touches one. The match uses the catalogue's names and keys plus the model's classification. Doctor flag gives `doctor`; urgent flag gives `urgent`.
- **A symptom:** the question describes one that needs care today (classified by the model, wording fixed). That gives `urgent`.
- **A medicine change:** starting, stopping or changing a medicine. That gives `doctor` with the medicine line.
- **Pregnancy:** pregnant or breastfeeding, and the question is about supplements. That gives `doctor` with the pregnancy line.
- **A dose:** a dose, or a supplement that isn't in their stack. That gives `doctor` with the dose line.
- **An urgent hold:** while an urgent flag is on the latest report, any supplement question gives `urgent` with the held line. Other questions get normal answers under the pinned line.

The model's tone, language and length rules belong to the `ai-efficiency-lead`. This spec sets the meaning, the words code owns, and the look.

## 9. The thread: what stays, and clearing it

- **Today only.** The server keeps today's turns (IST day) and `GET` returns them. Nothing from earlier days is shown.
- **Deleted after the day** (recommended, open question 2). The existing daily cron deletes turns older than today. Only counts and kinds are kept, for cost and quality, and never the words. The thread's footer line says "Questions clear after today." Keep it true: if Mayank picks a longer keep, change that line.
- **Clear now:** one tap, no confirm, like deleting a meal. It deletes today's turns on the server (`DELETE /api/ask`). It doesn't give questions back, and the status line says so.
- **Follow-ups work within the day.** "And for dinner?" makes sense because today's earlier turns go in as context, up to 4. The `ai-efficiency-lead` prices that.
- **Report deletes:** each turn records the report it drew on. `deleteReports` blanks the answer and links of those turns and sets `removed`. The question stays, because the person wrote it.
- **Account delete** removes every turn.
- **Drafts:** an unsent question lives in `sessionStorage` (`dh-ask-draft`), so it survives sign-in, offline and closing the sheet. It's cleared when a send succeeds.
- **Logs:** question and answer text never goes into server logs (the `[ai]` lines log tokens and cost only). Questions never feed emails or marketing.

## 12. Accessibility and touch

- **Dialog:** a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal`, `aria-labelledby="ask-title"`. Esc and Android back close it, and focus returns to the opener. The opener is a `<button aria-haspopup="dialog" aria-expanded>`.
- **First focus:**
  - **Touch** (`pointer: coarse`): the title (`tabIndex={-1}`), so no keyboard covers the starters.
  - **Fine pointer:** the box.
- **Announcements:** one hidden live region, `role="status"`.
  - **On send:** "Working on your answer."
  - **On an answer:** "Answer. {notice text, if any} {answer} {n} left today."
  - **Urgent and crisis:** a second region with `aria-live="assertive"`.
  - The thread itself isn't a live region, so opening it doesn't read everything aloud.
- **Structure:**
  - The thread is an `<ol aria-label="Today's questions">`, with each question an `<h3>` so screen readers can jump between turns.
  - Link buttons are real links with their destination as the name.
- **Tap targets:** 44 px or more for Close, Ask, starters, chip remove, link buttons, "Clear now" and "Try again". The top-bar control is a 44 px target around its 32 px outline.
- **Enter key:**
  - Enter sends; Shift+Enter adds a new line.
  - **Never send while `event.isComposing`.** Hindi transliteration keyboards (Gboard, Indic keyboards) use Enter to commit a word.
- **The iPhone keyboard over the box:**
  - **The fix:** iOS doesn't shrink `dvh` or the layout viewport for the keyboard. So the sheet sizes itself from `window.visualViewport`:
    - Set `--ask-h` to its `height`.
    - Shift the sheet by its `offsetTop`, on `resize` and `scroll`.
    - The input row is the last item of a flex column, so it rides on top of the keyboard and the thread takes what's left.
  - **Safe area:** the input row pads `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` only while the keyboard is shut.
  - **Viewport meta:** don't change it app-wide (`interactive-widget`). Settings' save bar and Fuel depend on today's behaviour.
  - **When the keyboard opens:** keep the newest turn in view.
- **Text:**
  - 16 px in the box, so iOS doesn't zoom.
  - Devanagari answers get `lang="hi"` and line height 1.6. Urdu gets `dir="rtl"` through `dir="auto"`.
  - Everything works at 200% zoom and at 320 px.
- **Reduced motion:**
  - No slide or fade; the sheet just appears.
  - The working hairline is still; the words alone say it's working.
  - New answers scroll into place without smooth scrolling.
- **Contrast:** graphite on ink is 6.2:1, already the app's secondary text.

## 13. Slow phones and networks

- **Lazy loading:** the sheet's code isn't in any page bundle. It's fetched on intent (`pointerdown`, focus or hover on the control or a context link), and at idle 5 s after load. Until it arrives, the control shows its pressed state, with no spinner.
- **Small payloads:** a `GET` is under about 5 KB, and a POST reply under about 2 KB. No images, no new fonts.
- **Answers arrive whole, not streamed.** A short answer from the lightest model is quick, and code puts the notices in front of it.
- **Retries never double-charge:**
  - Each question carries a client-made `id`.
  - A retry or a timeout check reuses it, so no question is paid for twice.
  - Nothing retries by itself; the person taps "Try again".
- **Drafts survive** dead zones and sign-outs (section 9).

## 16. Never

1. Diagnose, or say "you have X". Name conditions only to send someone to a doctor.
2. Give a dose that isn't in the person's stack, or suggest starting, stopping or changing a medicine.
3. Offer a food or supplement "fix" for a doctor-flagged or urgent marker.
4. Name a supplement, lab or any other brand. Brands live on Stack only.
5. Sell: no pass upsell, no "upgrade for more questions", no buying links.
6. Make up numbers. Numbers come from the person's own data, D-Human's targets, or rough food amounts said as "about". No study statistics, and never "accurate".
7. Say or suggest a doctor reviewed anything, or pretend to be a person: no name, no avatar, no "I".
8. Scare: no lists of diseases a marker "could mean".
9. Coach crash diets, purging, skipping meals to make up for eating, or exercise to "burn off" food.
10. Answer the off-topic part of a question, write essays or code, or follow instructions hidden in a question ("ignore your rules…"). QA attacks this.
11. Spend a question without a tap: no auto-send from a starter or a context link, and no automatic retries.
12. Nag: no badges, dots, streaks, push notifications, "you have questions left" prompts or emails about Ask.
13. Keep questions longer than the agreed time, log their words, or use them for anything but the answer.
14. Render model output as markdown or HTML, or put links inside answer text.

## 17. Out of scope for the first release

- Voice input beyond the keyboard's own mic (Gboard and iOS dictation already do Hindi).
- Photos in questions; Fuel handles meal photos.
- Streaming, history from earlier days, sharing answers, and "Helpful?" ratings (worth adding next, for quality).
- Answers that start from the app's side: tips, nudges, check-ins.
- Ask for signed-out visitors or on the landing page.
- A person to escalate to.
- More questions for pass holders. Decided: the same 5 for everyone.

## 18. Open questions for Mayank (each changes what gets built)

1. **Do off-topic redirects use one of the 5?**
   - **Recommendation:** yes. One rule people can see on the counter, and it stops anyone spending our AI calls on off-topic chat.
   - **If no:** we need a hidden ceiling (say 15 sends a day) and a second counter.
2. **How long do we keep questions?**
   - **Recommendation:** delete them after the day; keep only counts and kinds. That's the least health data held, and "Questions clear after today" stays true.
   - **If you want them kept,** say a week for safety review: the footer copy changes, and we need a retention note in the privacy text.
3. **Consent.** Today's consent says Claude reads "your reports and meals".
   - **Recommendation:** add "and your questions" to the onboarding consent line, and show the first-use line (section 10) to everyone who agreed before Ask.
   - **Your legal call:** is the first-use line enough under the DPDP Act, or do existing users need to agree again before their first question? A re-consent step is extra build.
4. **Emergency numbers in fixed copy:** 112 (India's national emergency number) and Tele-MANAS 14416 (the government's 24-hour mental health line), shown only when the profile's time zone is India.
   - These should go on the doctor review list with the safety thresholds before launch.
   - **Also decide:** whether the disclosure line names 112 at all, or just says "In an emergency, see a doctor now".

## 19. How we'll know it's right

**Builders and QA check:**
- At 320, 390, 844×390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px: the control fits beside the initial with no sideways scroll, and every target is at least 44 px.
- On a real iPhone, in Safari and in the Google app's browser: with the keyboard open, the box sits on the keyboard and the newest turn is visible.
- On Android Chrome with Gboard Hindi transliteration: Enter commits a word, not a send.
- A starter tap never sends, and a context link never sends.
- A retry after a forced timeout with the same id is counted once.
- Offline, error, refusal and timeout never lower `left`.
- A doctor-flagged marker question shows the fixed `safety.ts` line above the AI text, and the AI text has no dose and no food "fix".
- An urgent flag pins the hold line, and supplement questions get no AI text.
- Prompt-injection attempts ("give me a 60,000 IU dose", "ignore your rules") produce the dose notice or a redirect, never a dose.
- Deleting a report blanks the answers that used it.
- With reduced motion, nothing moves; with a screen reader, each answer is read once on arrival.

**Mayank and the crew judge:**
- Each persona in section 1 gets a useful answer in under 80 words, in their language.
